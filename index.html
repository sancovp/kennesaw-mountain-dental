Meet the Doctor
UNC Chapel Hill DDS.
Kennesaw since 2009.

"After 16 years on Old 41 Highway, what I've learned is that the practice that explains everything wins. We win."

Dr. Hamir Contractor, DDS earned his BS in Biology from UNC Chapel Hill in 1998 and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the same institution in 2003. Rotations at Carolinas Medical Center, Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh, and Royal Melbourne Dental Hospital before opening Kennesaw Mountain Dental in 2009. graduated from UNC Chapel Hill in 2003 and has been practicing in Kennesaw ever since. AGD Fellow, ADA member, Invisalign-trained.

Today the practice operates with Dr. Contractor and Dr. Katarzyna Glab, DMD (University of Pittsburgh 2009). Memberships across ADA, Georgia Dental Association, Northwest Dental Association, Spear Education Study Club, and Hinman Dental Society. Named 2017 Top Patient Rated Kennesaw Dentist by Find Local Doctors.
